# Electronics Learning Hub

A **beginner-friendly electronics, circuits & embedded-systems learning resource**. Pick a
board — **Arduino (AVR), ESP32, or a general microcontroller** — and learn with beginner-first
tutorials, hands-on projects, a curated bookmark library, a glossary with tooltips, and a
difficulty ladder. The full learning wiki lives in BookStack (`electronics.sektor9.dev`).

## 🧭 Choose your path
Pick one board and follow its whole track — you don't need to read everything:

- [**Arduino (AVR)**](paths/arduino/index.md) — 8-bit AVR in C, the friendliest entry point.
- [**ESP32 (Xtensa/RISC-V)**](paths/esp32/index.md) — dual-core IoT SoC, C/C++/MicroPython.
- [**Microcontrollers (general)**](paths/mcu/index.md) — the core concepts that apply to every board.
- [**Circuits & Schematics**](paths/circuits/index.md) — the electronics that make it all work.

## 📚 What's here
- **Core concepts** — electricity, Ohm's law, components, circuits, microcontrollers, projects.
- **Synth. tutorials** — Arduino (C), ESP32 (C/C++), Rust on embedded, MicroPython.
- **Curated resource library** — a big bookmark bar of free tutorials, docs, videos and communities.
- **Glossary** — every term linked with a hover-tooltip and a detailed page.
- **Wiki** — the full library of books/chapters lives in BookStack at **`electronics.sektor9.dev`**.
