# Circuit Design & Test

> **Level:** 🟢 Beginner → 🟠 Intermediate · **Est. time:** ~8 weeks · **Progress:** track your checkboxes below

**What you'll learn:** The electronics lab skills — reading schematics, using test gear, troubleshooting, and building circuits that actually work the first time.

**Who it's for:** Beginners who've learned the theory and now want the hands-on skills of a real electronics bench.

---

## How this path works

Follow the steps **in order**. Tick a checkbox when you finish a step.

**Legend:** 🟢 Beginner · 🟠 Intermediate · 🔴 Hard · ⚫ Advanced

---

## 🟢 Stage 1 — Lab basics (weeks 1-3)

- [ ] **1. The multimeter** · 🟢 · 📝 [Multimeter use (SparkFun)](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/how-to-use-a-multimeter) — the first tool: measuring voltage, current, resistance, continuity.
- [ ] **2. Soldering basics** · 🟢 · 📝 [Soldering tutorial (Adafruit)](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-guide-excellent-soldering) — through-hole then SMD; it's a skill, practice it.
- [ ] **3. Electronics safety** · 🟢 · 📝 [EEVblog — safety video](https://www.youtube.com/@EEVblog) — mains, capacitors, and the habits that keep you safe.
- [ ] **4. Reading a schematic** · 🟢 · 📝 [How to read schematics](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/) — symbols, nets, and power/ground conventions.

## 🟠 Stage 2 — Test & troubleshoot (weeks 4-6)

- [ ] **5. The oscilloscope** · 🟠 · 📝 [w2aew's oscilloscope tutorials](https://www.youtube.com/@w2aew) — the scope: trigger, probes, and reading signals.
- [ ] **6. The logic analyzer** · 🟠 · 📝 [Saleae logic analyzer tutorials](https://www.saleae.com/) — capture I²C, SPI, and UART to see what your MCU is really sending.
- [ ] **7. Troubleshooting circuits** · 🟠 · 📝 [Troubleshooting (SparkFun)](https://learn.sparkfun.com/tutorials/how-to-troubleshoot-circuits) — systematic debugging: check power first, then signals, then components.

## 🟠 Stage 3 — Build & measure (weeks 7-8)

- [ ] **8. Signal integrity basics** · 🟠 · 📝 [Signal integrity for beginners](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/) — noise, grounding, decoupling, and why scope probes matter.
- [ ] **9. A full bench project** · 🟠 · 🎬 [The Signal Path](https://www.youtube.com/@TheSignalPath) — see how a pro designs, builds, and measures RF/analog hardware.
- [ ] **10. Document your work** · 🟠 · 📝 [Schematic capture in KiCad](https://docs.kicad.org/) — turn your breadboard into a clean, shareable schematic.

---

## 🎯 Milestone project

Put it together: **build a circuit, then fully characterize it** — design it on the breadboard, measure its waveforms with a scope, find and fix one deliberate bug, and produce a clean KiCad schematic + a short test report.
