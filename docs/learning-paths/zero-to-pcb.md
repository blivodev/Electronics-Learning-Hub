# Electronics Zero to PCB

> **Level:** 🟢 Beginner → 🔴 Hard · **Est. time:** ~12 weeks · **Progress:** track your checkboxes below

**What you'll learn:** Electronics from absolute zero — voltage/current/resistance, components, breadboard circuits, and finally designing and ordering your own PCB.

**Who it's for:** Complete beginners with zero electronics background who want to build real circuits and boards.

---

## How this path works

Follow the steps **in order**. Tick a checkbox when you finish a step. Steps are books 📚, tutorials 📝, or videos 🎬.

**Legend:** 🟢 Beginner · 🟠 Intermediate · 🔴 Hard · ⚫ Advanced

---

## 🟢 Stage 1 — The fundamentals (weeks 1-4)

- [ ] **1. Electricity basics** · 🟢 · 📝 [Electricity & Voltage/Current/Resistance](https://elecdocs.sektor9.dev/concepts/01-electricity/) — voltage, current, resistance, and the water-analogy mental model.
- [ ] **2. Ohm's Law & components** · 🟢 · 📝 [Ohm's Law & Components](https://elecdocs.sektor9.dev/concepts/02-components/) — resistors, LEDs, capacitors, transistors, and the resistor color code.
- [ ] **3. Lessons in Electric Circuits** · 🟢 · 📚 [All About Circuits — free textbook](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/textbook/) — the complete free electronics textbook (Kuphaldt).
- [ ] **4. Ben Eater's electronics** · 🟢 · 🎬 [Ben Eater — 8-bit computer playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLowKtXNTBypGqImE405J2565dvjafglHU) — see the theory become real hardware on a breadboard.

## 🟠 Stage 2 — Breadboards & circuits (weeks 5-8)

- [ ] **5. Circuits & schematics** · 🟠 · 📝 [Circuits & Schematics](https://elecdocs.sektor9.dev/concepts/03-circuits/) — series/parallel, reading a schematic, the breadboard.
- [ ] **6. From breadboard to board** · 🟠 · 📝 [From Breadboard to Board](https://elecdocs.sektor9.dev/concepts/05-projects/) — your first real project structure.
- [ ] **7. GreatScott!'s builds** · 🟠 · 🎬 [GreatScott!](https://www.youtube.com/@greatscottlab) — real project builds with sound theory and debugging.
- [ ] **8. Oscilloscope & multimeter** · 🟠 · 📝 [EEVblog's oscilloscope tutorials](https://www.youtube.com/@EEVblog) — learn the two tools that reveal what circuits actually do.

## 🔴 Stage 3 — Design a real PCB (weeks 9-12)

- [ ] **9. KiCad basics** · 🔴 · 📝 [PCB Design with KiCad](https://elecdocs.sektor9.dev/synthesized/pcb-design-kicad/) — schematic → footprint → board → Gerbers in KiCad.
- [ ] **10. KiCad docs** · 🔴 · 📚 [KiCad Documentation](https://docs.kicad.org/) — the official manual for when the tutorial isn't enough.
- [ ] **11. Phil's Lab PCB series** · 🔴 · 🎬 [Phil's Lab](https://www.youtube.com/@PhilsLab) — professional-grade PCB design in KiCad, start to finish.
- [ ] **12. Order and test your board** · 🔴 · 📝 [PCB design tutorial](https://www.alternatezone.com/electronics/files/PCBDesignTutorialRevA.pdf) — design rules, then order from a fab and assemble.

---

## 🎯 Milestone project

Put it together: **design a small PCB in KiCad** (e.g. an ATtiny or ESP32 breakout with an LED + button), order it, solder it, and get it working. This is the "I'm a hardware engineer now" milestone.
