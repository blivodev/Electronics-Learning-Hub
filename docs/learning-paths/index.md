# 🧭 Learning Paths

Not sure where to start? Pick a **learning path**: a curated, ordered sequence that walks you from beginner to advanced through the hub's books, tutorials, and videos. Follow the steps in order, tick them off as you go, and the progress tracker keeps your place.

Each step has a **difficulty badge**:

- 🟢 **Beginner** — no prior knowledge assumed
- 🟠 **Intermediate** — some programming / basic hardware background
- 🔴 **Hard** — comfortable with the fundamentals
- ⚫ **Advanced / Very Hard** — production-grade systems

> 💡 **How to use:** open a path, start at step 1, and don't skip. Tick each step's checkbox when you finish — your progress saves in your browser. Each path is also mirrored in the wiki as a "Path" book.
