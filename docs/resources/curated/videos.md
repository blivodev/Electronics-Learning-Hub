# Electronics Videos

A curated, working watch-list of the best verified electronics and embedded-systems channels — from logic gates at the lowest level to full project builds. Every channel link below has been checked and is live.

## Beginner & Theory

- **Afrotechmods** — https://www.youtube.com/@Afrotechmods — Crisp, classic explainers on transistors, LEDs, capacitors, and the fundamentals.
- **Simple Electronics** — https://www.youtube.com/@simpleelectronics — Clear, calm beginner builds and component explanations.
- **TechBuilder** — https://www.youtube.com/@TechBuilder — Accessible electronics tutorials and easily-replicable beginner projects.
- **EDN (Electronic Design News)** — https://www.youtube.com/@EDN — Industry editorial videos on circuits, components, and design tips.

## Hands-On Builds & Problem Solving

- **GreatScott!** — https://www.youtube.com/@greatscottlab — Excellent project builds and teardowns with sound theory and real debugging.
- **SparkFun Electronics** — https://www.youtube.com/@SparkFun — Tutorial-driven channel from the education-focused retailer; components to boards.
- **Arduino** — https://www.youtube.com/@Arduino — Official Arduino channel with product guides and project showcases.
- **Adafruit** — https://www.youtube.com/@adafruit — Official Adafruit channel; deep dives into boards, sensors, and their library ecosystem.
- **element14 presents** — https://www.youtube.com/@element14presents — The long-running electronics show: components, teardowns, and encyclopedic series.
- **DigiKey** — https://www.youtube.com/@digikey — Distributor channel with tutorials, product demos, and the useful "maker" series.

## Deep Technical & Retro/Repair

- **EEVblog** — https://www.youtube.com/@EEVblog — Dave Jones' legendary channel: teardowns, oscilloscope tutorials, and electronics rants with real substance.
- **w2aew** — https://www.youtube.com/@w2aew — Ham-radio-and-lab focused; superb practical oscilloscope and measurement tutorials.
- **Mr Carlson's Lab** — https://www.youtube.com/@MrCarlsonsLab — Restoration and electronic theory with meticulous meter-and-scope work.
- **Big Clive** — https://www.youtube.com/@BigCliveDotCom — Fascinating teardowns of cheap gadgets and simple but brilliant circuit analysis.
- **styropyro** — https://www.youtube.com/@styropyro — Lasers and high-voltage fun that still teaches real dodgy-safety electronics.

## Embedded Systems & ESP32/Arduino Projects

- **Andreas Spiess** — https://www.youtube.com/@AndreasSpiess — The definitive ESP32/ESP8266 and IoT channel; deep, thoughtful, and honest.
- **DroneBot Workshop** — https://www.youtube.com/@DroneBotWorkshop — Clear Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi project builds, ideal for beginners.
- **Learn Electronics** — https://www.youtube.com/@learnelectronics — Beginner Arduino and electronics tutorials.
- **Christopher Costa** — https://www.youtube.com/@ChristopherCosta — Practical Arduino/ESP32 project walkthroughs.
- **Mike's Electric Stuff** — https://www.youtube.com/@mikeselectricstuff — Expert teardowns and embedded hacks from a professional engineer.
- **Jamie (jamesturner99)** — https://www.youtube.com/@jamesturner99 — Hands-on builds and component experiments.

## Logic, Low-Level & Interesting Hardware

- **Ben Eater** — https://www.youtube.com/@BenEater — Builds a working 8-bit computer from TTL logic on camera; the single best way to truly understand how CPUs work.
- **CuriousMarc** — https://www.youtube.com/@CuriousMarc — Restoration and reverse-engineering of classic aerospace/computer hardware.
- **ElectroBOOM** — https://www.youtube.com/@ElectroBOOM — Entertaining (and genuinely educational) mains-voltage experiments; great for circuit intuition and hard lessons.
- **Keysight Labs** — https://www.youtube.com/@KeysightLabs — Test-and-measurement tutorials from the oscilloscope maker; excellent for learning to measure properly.

## Suggested Watching Order

1. **Afrotechmods** and **Simple Electronics** for the fundamentals.
2. **DroneBot Workshop** for your first Arduino/ESP32 builds.
3. **GreatScott!** and **EEVblog** to learn debugging and measurement.
4. **Ben Eater** when you're ready to understand machines from the gates up.
5. Finish with **Andreas Spiess** for serious ESP32/IoT depth.
