## Suggested watching order

1. **Afrotechmods** and **ElectroBOOM 101** for the fundamentals.
2. **DroneBot Workshop** and **Paul McWhorter** for your first Arduino/ESP32 builds.
3. **EEVblog** and **w2aew** to learn debugging and measurement with an oscilloscope.
4. **Ben Eater** when you're ready to understand machines from the gates up.
5. Finish with **Andreas Spiess** and **Phil's Lab** for serious ESP32/embedded and PCB depth.
